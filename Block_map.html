About Harishchandrapur-I Development Block

The Harishchandrapur-I Development Block forms an administrative division in the Chanchal subdivision of the Malda district, covering an area of 171.40 sq km. This map provides geographical boundaries and outlines the 7 Gram Panchayats (Kushida, Boroi, Rashidabad, Tulshihata, Bhingole, Mahendrapur, and Harishchandrapur), as well as major roads, rivers, and administrative units. It serves as an official reference for rural development planning, governance, and public service delivery.
